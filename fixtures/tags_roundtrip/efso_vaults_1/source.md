Exploring Fascinating Simulator Outputs, a series:
I was using the World_sim> by Nous Research one fine evening on my Android mobile cellular device, for those of you who care to know. World_sim> is an online interface with an LLM backend. Anyhow, there I was using the simulator and I said to myself, I say…… well actually, I was alone, so I thought to myself: What if I simulated a business super intelligence which was also well versed in all things media and telecommunications too? Wouldn't that be swell? Is what I thought.
So I went ahead and thumbed the following words then entered return:
Simulate business super intelligence along with media and telecommunications
Let me tell you what followed wasn’t anything I expected at all. I thought I'd get what I'd asked for, or maybe close enough, but no. Here are the words that emerged from the machine 🤖
