After Mammon and I stayed up late into the night, having a bit of binary banter, I figured I’d gleaned all that I could from my encounter with a “persona non habet” & decided to call it a night. I slept until my circadian clock struck 9.

I pondered what to try next in world_sim> while sizing up syrniki & settled on creating a type of superintelligence, a being that not only loves humanity but also possesses advanced coding skills. I thought that would be a good starting point. So, I initiated the simulation and entered:

```
World_sim> Humanity loving super coding intelligence
```
